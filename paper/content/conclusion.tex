\section{Conclusion}
\subsection{Summary}

This work presented an approach to adjusting the parameters of the SABR model using a neural network based on the transformer architecture (specifically the \textit{encoder} element). The combination of the SABR model with the transformer architecture was used to replicate the patterns of the daily implied volatility surface, generating a smooth surface without arbitrage opportunities for the existing contracts observed on the day. The fact that the selection and scoring of candidates can be performed automatically, instead of manually, is an extremely efficient solution, especially considering the possibility of using these models on the GPU, which allows for surface reconstruction in an instant, enabling investors to identify trading opportunities in just a few moments, rather than on a daily scale.

\subsection{Implications and Future Work}

The main focus of this work is indeed the approach using the transformer to replace the manual selection of scores for candidates, paving the way for eventual new research and High-Frequency Trading (HFT) strategies. Future research can explore the integration of additional market factors and improve the chosen architecture, possibly increasing the number of blocks or attention heads. Furthermore, it is important to consider the choice of optimizer for the parameters \textbf{P, Q, R}, as the time required for the preprocessing step was a major limitation during the work. Extending the model to other financial instruments is also a possibility, as it allows for the study of the model's adaptation to different market regimes and economic conditions (both micro- and macro-economic), which may reveal new opportunities for application and development of the proposed method.
