\title{Deep Implicit Layers for No-Arbitrage Implied Volatility Surface Interpolation}

\author{

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
    We implement a neural network architecture based on deep implicit layers with the objective of optimizing the parameters of
    multiple SABR (stochastic alpha, beta, rho) models in the context of financial options.
    Our network is trained on a dataset of historical traded option contracts and their respective calculated implied volatility values.
    We perform a candidate selection process using summarized suitability scores to remove possible outliers from our candidate set,
    aiming to minimize arbitrage opportunities when optimizing for the SABR model parameters.
    Our approach leverages the implicit formulation for the scoring process that solves for an implicit fixed-point problem
    between the observed data and SABR generated surfaces,
    where a transformer architecture is combined to deep implicit layers to process sequences of contracts with varying lengths.
    The surfaces produced with our scoring network are then compared to a baseline linear interpolation of the daily surfaces.
\end{abstract}
